Disclosures

How we are paid, and what we are not.

Insurance

Fortune U Group solicits insurance only for insurers where a valid appointment / code exists: life — LIC, HDFC Life; health — Care Health, Niva Bupa, Tata AIG, ICICI Lombard. We are not a broker for the whole market. Product ads that name a policy, benefit or premium need that insurer’s written approval.

Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation. Policy issuance, claims and servicing are the insurer’s responsibility.

Mutual funds

Distribution requires a valid AMFI ARN after NISM Series V-A. Until ARN appears in js/config.js and in the footer, we do not solicit or distribute mutual fund units. After ARN we will sell regular plans only and display “AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor” with the ARN on this website, cards and WhatsApp status material.

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme-related documents carefully. We will not publish projected or guaranteed returns.

Not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser

We do not charge a fee for investment advice. We do not use the words adviser, advisor, IFA or wealth manager for this business. Goal conversations are part of product solicitation / distribution, not SEBI RIA services.

Commission

Insurers and (after ARN) AMCs pay us commission. If you ask, we will tell you the commission scale for the product being discussed. You can buy the same insurance or a direct-plan mutual fund without us.

Complaints

Insurance grievances: the insurer’s grievance cell, then Bima Bharosa / IGMS on irdai.gov.in. Mutual fund grievances: the AMC, then AMFI / SCORES after we have an ARN.

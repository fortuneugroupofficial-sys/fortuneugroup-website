About

A product distributor, not a SEBI adviser.

We help families in Tirupati buy insurance from the insurers that appointed us, and — after AMFI ARN — invest in regular-plan mutual funds. We are paid by product manufacturers, not by a planning fee.

Why we exist

Many first-time buyers are sold the wrong cover or a SIP they do not understand. Fortune U Group was set up to keep the conversation simple: what you need, what the product actually does, and what we earn.

We do not manage a fund, underwrite a policy, or give investment advice for a fee. The insurer and the AMC carry those roles.

Mission

Solicit only licensed products, explain them in Telugu or English, and stay available after the sale.

What we will not do

We will not call ourselves an investment adviser, wealth manager or IFA. We will not promise SIP returns. We will not advertise loans, Demat or credit cards until we hold the matching appointment.

Based in Tirupati

Andhra Pradesh. We also speak with families in nearby districts and Hyderabad over phone / WhatsApp.

Why this cover

Protect the corpus

Without cover, families break SIPs and gold. Health insurance is for the hospital bill. LIC / HDFC Life term is for income. Different jobs.

80D, if you pay tax

Premiums may qualify under Section 80D. We are not tax advisers — confirm with your CA.

Typically covered

In-patient hospitalisation, pre/post hospitalisation as per the plan, day-care procedures, and modern treatments if the product includes them. Waiting periods apply for PEDs and specific illnesses.

Typically not a free lunch

OPD unless bought, cosmetic treatment, non-payable consumables unless the plan says so, and anything in the exclusion list. Always read the prospectus and policy wording.
